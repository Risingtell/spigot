# Code of Conduct

## The standard

Be straightforward and be kind. Assume good faith. Criticise code and reasoning,
not people. Disagreement is welcome and is most useful when it is specific.

Not acceptable: harassment of any kind, personal attacks, publishing anyone's
private information, or sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

Applies in this repository, its issues and pull requests, and anywhere someone is
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report anything to risingtell@gmail.com. Reports are handled privately.
Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions that do not meet this
standard, and may block persistent offenders.
